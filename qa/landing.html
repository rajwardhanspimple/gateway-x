how it works

Four moving parts, all yours

No third-party dashboard and no shared control plane. One Postgres schema, one edge function, and a console you host.

01

Store the origin once

Its base URL, auth scheme and keys go into your own Postgres. Nothing upstream is ever sent to a browser.

02

Publish your own ids

Map a public name like rs-core onto the upstream model. Callers only ever learn the left-hand side.

03

Issue gateway keys

Customers mint rs_live_ keys, call your endpoint with any OpenAI-compatible SDK, and get metered per request.

04

Watch key health

Each upstream key carries a working or failing state, updated from real traffic rather than a guess.

One endpoint, any OpenAI-compatible SDK

Swap the base URL and the key. Paths, request bodies and streaming stay exactly as your customers already wrote them.

  • Chat completions, streaming and embeddings pass straight through
